# Dungeon Depths

A tiny top-down action dungeon crawler — one file, no assets, everything drawn with code.

Play it live: **https://\<yourname\>.github.io/dungeon-depths/**

## How to play
- **Move** — WASD / Arrow keys (step, tile by tile)
- **Attack** — X or Spacebar: tap for the 3-hit sword combo (2 / 4 / 4 damage); hold ~1 second for the charged slam (5 damage, 3-tiles-wide, 2-tile knockback)
- **Mute** — M · **Restart** — ENTER

Clear every monster on a floor to unlock the stairs. Clearing a floor scores
100 × floor number; your best run is saved in your browser.

## Monsters
- **Slimes** — split in two when big
- **Zombies** — sturdy shufflers
- **Skeletons** — keep their distance and shoot; pin them down
- **Spiders** — crawl straight through walls

Watch the red tile glow: that's where the next hit lands.
